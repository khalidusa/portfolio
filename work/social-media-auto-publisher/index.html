Social media auto-publisher

The publishing half of the content system. Finished videos land in a Dropbox folder and go out on schedule to Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts, one a day per platform, with no one logging in to post. Every week it also writes and designs branded image posts and holds them 48 hours for review before scheduling them.

  • Quality checks on every file (format, length, audio, size), with a written reason for anything rejected
  • Per-platform rules and queues, so a video that does not fit one platform never leaves a gap on the others
  • Runs on GitHub Actions every 3 hours with a permanent ledger of every post, at almost no running cost
