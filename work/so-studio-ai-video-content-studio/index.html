SO STUDIO: AI video content studio

I built SO STUDIO to keep up with the volume of video social platforms demand. One long video or podcast goes in; finished, on-brand Shorts, Reels, TikToks and episodes come out. It runs fully on a Mac and uses only rights-free footage, music and effects, so ads are not blocked or muted.

  • Every format from one upload, with speaker tracking, captions, motion graphics, music ducking and filler removal
  • Rights-free by design: AI-checked Pexels B-roll, licensed music and CC0 sound effects
  • Brand kits from any website, per-brand compliance rules, and AI-written posts sent to a scheduler after review
